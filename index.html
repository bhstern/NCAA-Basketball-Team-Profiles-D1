About This Project

This started as an open-ended exploration. I was watching games every week, taking detailed notes, and I wanted to know if what the data said matched what my eyes were telling me. That question eventually turned into this site.

I built the entire tool from scratch — Python for the data pipeline and variable engineering, HTML and JavaScript for the front end, deployed on GitHub Pages. It combines Bart Torvik efficiency metrics with shot quality variables, PPP differentials, and residual performance layers I engineered myself across 11 seasons of D1 data covering 2016 through 2026. Every team profile includes D1-wide percentiles, conference context, and power vs. mid-major comparisons, all calculated independently.

The goal was never to just surface more stats. It was to turn data into something a coaching staff can actually use to understand their own identity, prepare for opponents, and make faster decisions. This is a proof of concept of what I can build, and I know the real value multiplies with access to private data like Synergy Sports and Second Spectrum.

Next up is individual player profiles, transfer portal decision support, and eventually clustering and similarity models, with a long-term goal of building predictive tools on the player development side.

Data Sources & Citation

Raw team statistics, adjusted efficiencies, roster metrics, and shooting splits were sourced from Bart Torvik's T-Rank database (barttorvik.com). All feature engineering — including shot profile construction, per-possession estimates, expected PPP (xPPP), shotmaking residuals, and descriptive layers (z-scores, percentiles, ranks) — was developed independently as part of this analytics pipeline.

Torvik, Bart. T-Rank College Basketball Statistics. barttorvik.com.

What Comes from Torvik

Adjusted efficiencies (adj_oe, adj_de, adj_em), tempo (adj._t, raw_t), Four Factors, roster metrics (height, experience, talent, SOS), and raw shooting splits by shot type are sourced directly from T-Rank.

What Was Engineered Independently

Combined rim metrics (off/def_rim_rate_total, off/def_rim_fg), the full-season PPP system (ppp_off_full, ppp_def_full), expected PPP (xPPP_off, xPPP_def) based on shot distribution multiplied by league-average shot values by year, shotmaking residuals (ppp_residual_off, ppp_residual_def), PPP and residual differentials, and all descriptive layers (z-scores, percentiles, D1 ranks, conference ranks, power/mid-major ranks) were built independently.

Dataset Scope

11 seasons of D1 college basketball (2016–2026). Approximately 3,900 team-season records. 430 columns per record including raw stats, engineered features, and descriptive layers. Updated through the 2025–26 full season.
